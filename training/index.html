Common questions

How long does it take?

Most Quick-Start games are ready to review in about two weeks once we have your material.

What do I need to give you?

Whatever you have: a handbook, a checklist, a few photos, or just a conversation about what goes wrong. We do the writing.

Do my employees need an account or an app?

No. It's a web link that opens on any phone, tablet or computer.

Is it private?

Your game lives at a link only you share. It isn't listed anywhere or searchable.

Can it go in our existing training system?

Yes. If you already use a learning system, ask about our Starter and Full Training packages, which can be packaged for it.
